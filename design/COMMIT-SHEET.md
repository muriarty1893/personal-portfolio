# COMMIT-SHEET - Murat Eker Notes

## 1. Peak / Signature

The notes behave like an editorial reading page: a minimal fixed header (signature left, sun/sound right), one centered 740px sheet, and a quiet index of title + date that opens into a long-form article. The visitor should describe it as reading a printed essay, not browsing an app.

## 2. Color

Warm paper `#e8e7e3` with ink `#26251f`, muted `#6b675f`, faint `#8a857c`. No accent color; hierarchy comes from value, italic, and whitespace. Dark mode inverts to `#171614` / `#eceae4`.

## 3. Type

Serif: Newsreader for index titles and body copy (stands in for Valkyrie). Mono: DM Mono for UI chrome and the large title art (stands in for Commit Mono). Caveat for the handwritten signature logo.

## 4. Grid break

One centered 740px sheet on both routes. The article breaks the quiet with a large mono title composition and, in the featured note, one dark WIP panel holding the cursor-follow eye.

## 5. Motion budget

1. Theme and sound toggles are the only chrome interactions.
2. The EvilEye shader is the single continuous effect, scoped inside the WIP button.
3. No scroll-jacking, no reveal choreography.

## 6. Reflex check

a) A generic developer blog gets a dark theme, terminal type, glow, and a grid of cards.

b) An AI avoiding that makes an off-white editorial page with a serif headline and quiet beige cards.

c) This page copies a specific reference: warm paper, fixed micro-header, one centered sheet, oversized serif reading text, and a mono title composition. The structure, not decoration, carries it.

## 7. House tells broken

1. Sticky sidebar / reader-pane workspace removed; single-column sheet instead.
2. Staggered menu removed; fixed minimal header instead.
3. Category tabs, cards, and lab grid removed; index is a plain list of title + date.
